# Section C5

**Area:** `e2e/tests/liga/c5-*.spec.ts` · **helper file:** `e2e/helpers/c5.ts` · **app id:** `c5`

The admin corrects a finished match: the match goes back into play, or a warning says why it can't.

Read `e2e/sekcije/README.md` first: where you work, how tests are written here, what "done" means and the report.

## Flows to cover
- `tables.adminEdit` (league "E2E · Scorepad"): a finished match the admin edits (change a hand, delete a hand, add a hand).
- If after the edit nobody has 1001 and the table is from tonight with nothing after it: the match is open again at its table with the time it had left, and standings and ratings don't count it until it closes (c826481, DECISIONS #97).
- Otherwise: the match stays without a winner and the warning says why it can't reopen.
- Otključaj partiju on a tournament table stays the manual way.
