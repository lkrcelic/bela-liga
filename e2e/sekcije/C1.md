## Flows to cover
- **Admin, league "E2E · Novo kolo"** (`leagues.newRound`, 8 teams, no start time): Večer lige → pick the league → Novo kolo → Tko igra večeras → Kreiraj → Skica kola → Odobri sva → Objavi → Dnevni poredak shows the published round.
- **Players at a table of that round:** Tko igra (pick two players) → the scorepad: Upiši igru through Izaberi tko je zvao → Zvanja → Igra → Spremi; the hand is in the list with the right totals.
- **Correct a saved hand** (the 1bb39ea flow): tap the hand → Ispravi korak po korak → change the points → Spremi; the list and the score show the corrected hand, and the database has one hand, not two. Also on `tables.correction` (league "E2E · Scorepad").
- **Finish the match:** play to 1001 (on `tables.finishMatch`, one hand away) → Završi meč → a second, quick tap must not open a new hand entry (#74) → Meč 2 → … → Kraj kola.
- **After the table closes:** Dnevni poredak and Ukupni poredak show the result, and the players' ratings moved.
- Bela rules for the expected numbers: a hand is 162 game points; the caller must score more than the other team (game points + zvanja), otherwise pad; štiglja 252 : 0 plus zvanja (`src/app/_lib/bela/scoring.ts`).
